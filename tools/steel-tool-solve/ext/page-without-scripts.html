Steel exam solver type the question, check the words, write down what it prints

  1. Type the question exactly as printed, one question at a time, including its answer line (for example Pu = ____ kips or W____x____) and, for multiple choice, every choice on its own line. Then press Solve. The answer line tells the page what is asked.
  2. Under each answer is a short list: Check these values. Each line shows a value and the words of YOUR question it came from. If the words do not say what the label says, open Change a value and fix it.
  3. Copy by hand what is under WRITE THIS. The number for your blank is the line that starts ANSWER (when there is a line ANSWER FOR YOUR BLANK, that one). An orange block means the page needs something from you first. A red block means the AI helper disagrees: decide it before you write.

FIRST, ONCE: the assumptions printed on the cover page of your exam
Look at the first page of the exam for a list that starts like "Unless noted otherwise, assume: ..." (all loads given are service loads; Fy = 50 ksi; pinned support conditions; K = 1.0 for all columns; shear lag factor U = 1.0 ...). Type that whole list here, once, before your first question. The page uses it for every question, and it changes answers. If your exam has no such list, leave this empty.

What this page cannot do (read once)
  • It cannot see a figure. A number that is only drawn (hole positions, spans, a width) must be typed by you: the page asks about the quantities it found in your text and makes you tick that you looked at the figure.
  • The calculator does not do: beams that are not fully braced (Lb above 0), shear strength, deflection, bolt or weld strength, combined bending and compression, composite beams, overhangs and continuous beams. The page says so for a part that asks for one of them.
  • The page's reader can be wrong, and so can the AI helper. Every value shows the words of your text it came from: if the words do not say what the label says, change the box.
  • It writes out the calculator's steps and answer. It does not decide what your professor wants written: write only what is under WRITE THIS and what your question asks for.
